Project details

The project

Project completed during my training at Digital Campus Paris, in a team of three (“Studio Nova”). A fictional site for Élan, a multi-activity gym (yoga, Pilates, boxing, HIIT, strength, cardio) founded by Debbie Scotto. Goal: a clear site that makes visitors want to book a first class.

My role

Analysing the client's request, writing the specifications, creating personas, defining the site goals, impact mapping, task flow and user flow, choosing the “Urban Energy” guidelines with the client, then building the site in WordPress and writing the test plan before launch.

Tools

WordPress · Elementor · Canva (personas, goals, impact mapping, task flow, user flow, presentation) · teamwork and project follow-up.

My process

From the client's request to the live site, in nine steps.

  1. 1

    The request

    Analysing the client's brief: her activities, target audience and expectations.

  2. 2

    The specifications

    Formalising the need: goals, content, pages, features, constraints and schedule.

  3. 3

    The personas

    Two typical profiles to understand visitors' needs and frustrations.

  4. 4

    The goals

    Nine goals defined from the client's request and the personas.

  5. 5

    Impact mapping

    Linking each goal to the personas, the expected changes and the site features.

  6. 6

    Task flow & user flow

    Mapping each persona's path through the site, step by step, to the action: booking a class, contacting the studio.

  7. 7

    The brand guidelines

    Choosing the “Urban Energy” guidelines with the client: colours, typefaces, mood.

  8. 8

    The site

    Building the site in WordPress with Elementor, responsive on mobile, tablet and desktop.

  9. 9

    The test plan

    Testing every page and feature, logging bugs and fixing them before launch.

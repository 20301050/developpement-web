Project details

The project

Workshop project at Digital Campus Paris for the Fondation de l'Espace (Space Foundation), in partnership with Game in Society.

The challenge: make the Space Game Jam 99 more than a one-off event — a true editorial asset that gives the Foundation lasting visibility. Highlight: the awards ceremony on 7 July 2026 at the Cité des Sciences, during the Assises du NewSpace.

My role

  • Big idea: “Gaming as living proof that space is us”, slogan « Play Earth. Think Space. »
  • 3 personas: the student creator, the NewSpace decision-maker, the curious general public
  • Content plan before and during the event for Instagram and LinkedIn: poster, globe video, testimonials, cross-posts with sponsors, B2B announcements
  • Editorial calendar up to the SGJ2 launch in February 2027, KPIs and recommendations
  • Final client presentation (11 slides)

Tools

  • Canva (presentation and visuals)
  • Excel (strategy summary and editorial calendar)
  • Instagram and LinkedIn

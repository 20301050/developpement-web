Le Comptoir Vert

One-page site for an organic bulk grocery in Neuilly-sur-Marne: opening hours up front, product families, the shop and its short-supply-chain partners.

Project details

The project

Training project : build the website of an organic bulk grocery in Neuilly-sur-Marne, as requested by the owner.

His request: a one-page site, simple and quick to read. No detailed product pages, only product families. Visitors must see the opening hours right away, discover the shop and get to know its short-supply-chain partners.

My role

  • Analysing the owner's request and monitoring a local competitor
  • Structure one-page : opening hours at the top, product families, shop presentation, short-supply-chain partners, contact
  • Building the site in WordPress
  • Writing the test plan and testing every part of the site

Tools

  • WordPress
  • Test plan

What the owner wanted

Four clear needs, turned into a single page.

  1. 1

    Opening hours right away

    From the moment they arrive, customers know whether the shop is open, without searching.

  2. 2

    Product families

    No detailed catalogue: the main bulk product families to give an idea of the range.

  3. 3

    Getting to know the shop

    The spirit of the place, organic and bulk, to make people want to visit.

  4. 4

    Short-supply-chain partners

    Highlighting the local producers who supply the grocery.

Frequently asked questions
Answers to the questions I'm asked most often.




How much does a website cost?
A showcase site starts at €690, a WordPress site at €990, an online shop at €1,290 and an SEO audit at €390. The final price depends on the number of pages and features you need: after a free call, I send you a detailed quote with no commitment. Payment in 2 or 3 instalments possible.


How long does it take to build my website?
Usually 3 weeks for a showcase site and 4 weeks for a WordPress site, once I have your texts and photos. No texts yet? I can write them with you after a one-hour interview. The exact schedule is in the quote.


Will I be able to edit my website myself?
Yes, with a WordPress site: you change your texts, photos and opening hours without knowing any code. I'll show you how.


Will my website be visible on Google?
Yes, the SEO basics are included: titles, descriptions, speed, mobile version and page structure. To go further, I offer a full SEO audit.


Do you work with small local shops?
Yes. I'm based in Neuilly-sur-Marne (93330, France) and I create websites for small shops, craftspeople and independent businesses all over France: remotely, or in person in the Paris region.


Can you also help my physical shop?
Yes. With my years of experience in visual merchandising, I advise you on your shop window, store layout and product display, so that your shop and your website tell the same story.


I don't have a logo or any copy yet. Is that a problem?
No. I can help you create your brand guidelines (colours, fonts, mood) and write clear copy for your visitors.

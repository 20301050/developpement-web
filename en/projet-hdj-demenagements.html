Project details

The project

Site in progress for HDJ Déménagements, a removals company operating in France and Europe for private and business customers. Goal: present the packages (luxury, standard, economy), truck-with-driver rental, furniture lift and storage, and make visitors want to request a free quote. It will go live as soon as the client sends the photos.

My role

I created the logo, defined the site structure (menu, Packages, Trucks, Furniture lift, Storage, Supplies, Guide and Contact pages) and designed the layout. I offered the client several palettes; he approved navy and red, and I made revisions after each round of feedback.

Tools

HTML / CSS / JavaScript · AI (site and visuals) · GitHub Pages (live preview) · exchanges and approvals with the client.

About me

Hello, I'm Malgorzata.

I am a web developer and digital project manager, trained at Digital Campus Paris. I build clear, fast, well-ranked websites, from scoping to launch: showcase websites coded in HTML/CSS, WordPress sites, redesigns and SEO audits.

To me, a good website guides visitors effortlessly. In UX/UI design, I design simple journeys and pages that make people want to act, with a measurable goal: more enquiries, more bookings, more sales.

I useGenerative AI at every step (ideas, mockups, code, content) to move faster, while keeping a human eye on design and quality. French-Polish and bilingual, I work in both French and Polish.

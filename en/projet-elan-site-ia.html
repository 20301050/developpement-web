Project details

The project

A new website for Élan, a multi-activity gym in Paris (boxing, HIIT, cardio, strength training, Pilates, yoga). Eight pages — Home, Our classes, Schedule, Prices, Coaches, News, Contact and Legal notice — designed for desktop and mobile. Goal: make visitors want to book a first session.

My role

Project manager: I directed the AI from A to Z. I defined the pages and content, chose the gym photos and mood, enforced the “Urban Energy” guidelines, then tested every page and requested fixes until the final result.

Tools

Claude (AI) to build the pages · “Urban Energy” guidelines (navy, coral, burgundy) · Barlow and Barlow Condensed typefaces · desktop and mobile testing.

My process

From specifications to finished site, in eight steps.

  1. 1

    Scoping

    Taking on the client's needs and the “Urban Energy” guidelines: movement, freedom, progress.

  2. 2

    Site structure

    Defining the eight pages and the visitor's path to booking a trial session.

  3. 3

    Content

    Classes, coaches, schedule, prices, news: short, motivating copy tailored to the audience.

  4. 4

    Visuals

    Choosing the gym photos (boxing area, HIIT floor, yoga studio) and the photo gradients.

  5. 5

    Interactive pages

    Schedule filterable by class and coach, pricing FAQ, contact form.

  6. 6

    Mobile

    A mobile version of every page, with a menu that opens and closes.

  7. 7

    Adjustments

    Lightening the home page, moving nutrition into the news with a “how to eat well” article, reworking the colours.

  8. 8

    Testing

    Testing each page on desktop and mobile, logging issues and getting them fixed.

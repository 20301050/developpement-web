PolskaVoyage

Travel site built with AI to showcase my country, Poland: four cities, the food, hotels, and Smok the dragon to learn Polish. New version in the colours of the sonigo app.

Project details

The project

Showcasing my country, Poland, to French-speaking travellers, with four cities – Kraków, Warsaw, Gdańsk and Zakopane – the food and hotels.

New version (October 2026): the site uses the branding of sonigo, the local-guide app from our UX project, with its mascot Smok, the Kraków dragon, who teaches visitors Polish.

My role

  • Choice of cities, places, dishes and hotels, from my knowledge of the country
  • Redesign of the visual identity to match the sonigo app
  • A 12-page structure, including Smok's legend, the app and booking
  • Mobile version: ☰ menu, titles and images adapted to phones
  • Site built with AI, which I directed and corrected page by page, plus my own photos of Kraków

Tools

  • Generative AI to create the pages
  • HTML / CSS / JavaScript
  • Figma app mockup (group project)
  • GitHub Pages for hosting

Site content

Four cities, Smok the dragon and the sonigo app to plan the whole trip.

  1. 1

    4 cities

    Kraków, Warsaw, Gdańsk and Zakopane: what to see, tours with a local guide and useful Polish words.

  2. 2

    Smok's legend

    The story of the Wawel dragon, the fire-breathing statue and a photo wall for travellers.

  3. 3

    Talk with Smok

    A small chat where Smok gives travel words in Polish, with pronunciation.

  4. 4

    The sonigo app

    How it works in 4 steps, and the interactive app mockup to try right in the page.

  5. 5

    What to eat, where to stay

    Six Polish specialities and a selection of hotels in Kraków, Warsaw and Zakopane.

  6. 6

    Booking and contact

    Booking and contact forms, designed for phones.

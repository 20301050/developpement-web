The project

Digital Campus Paris case for a real client, through the agency A DISPO : a truck-with-driver rental company in the Paris region.

The finding: a poorly organised 35-page site, no page for businesses and little visibility on Google. The goal: gain visibility and attract new clients, especially companies.

My role

  • Technical SEO audit : 35 URLs and 9 templates analysed, 404 errors, duplicate pages, accessibility
  • Market analysis: PESTEL, SWOT, benchmark of 4 competitors and 4 personas
  • New site structure and keyword mapping, with a local semantic cocoon (Paris, 92, 93, 94)
  • Home page redesign and creation of the Business page
  • 3-month acquisition plan: Google Ads, Meta Ads and an e-mailing partnership with estate agencies, for €1,500
  • SMART goals and KPI dashboard

Tools

  • Screaming Frog, PageSpeed Insights
  • Ahrefs (competitor data)
  • Google Ads, Meta Ads
  • Brevo (e-mailing)
  • Canva
